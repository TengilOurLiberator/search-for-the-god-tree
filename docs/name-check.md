# Name-clash check, 2026-10-10

The world bible owed a wider search for clashes with existing works. This is the result of a web search of each name (not a trademark search). Full source links are in the session report that produced this; the main ones are below.

## Replace, or accept knowingly

| Name | Found | Lead's recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| **the Underroot** | A dungeon named Underroot in *Diablo IV*; a delve named The Underroot in *The Elder Scrolls Online*; an upcoming Steam game titled *Underroot* | Rename. It is the busiest name on our list and one use is a title in our own medium. Candidates (not yet checked): the Rootdark, the Taproot Deeps, the Hollow Under Huskhaven. Owner's choice; the names were approved in decision 0008 |

## Keep an eye on

| Name | Found | Guidance |
|---|---|---|
| Godtree | "God Tree" is a central object in *Naruto* (two words, a plot object, not a title); a 2026 fantasy novel series is titled *The God Tree* | Always one word. Never give our tree a fruit or an eye. The full title is clear: no game, book or film is called *Search for the Godtree*, and a Steam search for "godtree" found nothing |
| Cradle | Will Wight's *Cradle* novels (world name); areas called The Cradle in *Hollow Knight: Silksong* and *Destiny* | Fine for a shrine. Never in the title or as the world's name |
| Rootwarden | *World of Warcraft: Midnight* (2026) uses "Rootwarden" as a role and gear name | Acceptable; ours is a creature. Reconsider if it becomes marketing-facing |
| Thornback | Enemy names in *Hogwarts Legacy*, *Warhammer 40,000*, *Path of Exile*; also a real fish | Generic compound nobody owns. Keep |
| weaving, weavers | *The Wheel of Time* ("weaves", "tying off"); the Weavers in *Hollow Knight* | Lean on **knots** as our word for a spell. Never call a spell "a weave"; never say "tying off" |
| the Woken, the Rooted | Light overlaps only (a 2013 novel, an upcoming survival game titled *Rooted*, Roblox's *Deepwoken*) | Keep |
| motes | Common fantasy vocabulary (*Destiny*, *World of Warcraft*) | Keep |

## Clear

Search for the Godtree (full title), the Rootway, Huskhaven, the Cradle Meadows, the Windlow, the Saltglass Shallows, the Emberloft, the Stillwood, the Bole, Burrling, Barkbound.

## Not checked

- Trademark registers (do this for the title, "Godtree" and "Cradle" before any store listing).
- Japanese, Chinese and Korean sources, which matters most for "Godtree".
- Each franchise wiki name by name; absence of search hits is moderate evidence only.

## A note on the premise

A journey to a colossal tree echoes several existing works, including the one our brief names. It is common ground, and [canon/vision.md](../canon/vision.md) lists how we differ. Keep marketing away from any framing that resembles them.
