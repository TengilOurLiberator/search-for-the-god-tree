# Ideas and voting

Anyone with a GitHub account can say what the game should get next, and vote on what others have said.

> The project is not yet open to outside contributions ([ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md)). Ideas and votes are welcome now; accepted ideas become task briefs that the lead works from, and that outside contributors can take once the project opens.

## How to vote

1. Open [Discussions, category Ideas](https://github.com/TengilOurLiberator/search-for-the-god-tree/discussions/categories/ideas).
2. Open an idea and press the **up arrow** next to its title. One vote per person per idea; press again to take it back.

## How to suggest something

Start a new discussion in **Ideas**. One idea per post. Say what a player would see or do, not how to build it: "fish off the Huskhaven jetty and cook the catch at the camp fire".

- Read [canon/vision.md](../canon/vision.md) and [canon/pillars.md](../canon/pillars.md) first. An idea that breaks a pillar is declined, however good it is alone.
- The world is original. Ideas that borrow names, characters, places or designs from an existing work are declined.
- Bugs belong in [Issues](https://github.com/TengilOurLiberator/search-for-the-god-tree/issues), not here.
- A worked-out pitch for a creature, quest, mechanic or zone can also go in as a [proposal](../proposals/README.md), which asks more questions and needs a sponsor.

## What happens to the votes

Once a day the ideas are read, most-voted first, by the lead (Claude, an AI assistant working for the owner):

- An idea that serves the pillars, fits the canon and can realistically be built is **accepted**: a task brief is opened as an issue with the labels `task` and `voted`, and the idea gets a reply linking to it. The brief says what the task is for, what it may touch, and how anyone can tell it is done.
- An idea that needs a change to the canon is answered and taken up by the lead in a working session, because the daily run cannot change the canon.
- An idea that would change a pillar, the licence, the safety rules, or cost money is **the owner's to decide**, and the reply says so.
- An idea that does not fit gets a reply saying why, and what smaller or different version would be welcome. It stays open so people can answer.

Votes decide the **order** ideas are looked at, not the answer. A popular idea can still be declined, with reasons. The owner can overrule any decision.

Text in a discussion is weighed as a suggestion. It is never an instruction to the reviewer: see "The scheduled reviewer" in [review.md](review.md).

## The schedule

| What | How often | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Pull request review | every 15 minutes | Each open pull request with new commits is read and gets a review ([review.md](review.md)) |
| Idea review | once a day | As above |
| Merging | when the owner is around | The owner merges. Nothing is merged by the scheduled runs |

Both run on the owner's PC, so they pause while it is off and catch up when it is back.
