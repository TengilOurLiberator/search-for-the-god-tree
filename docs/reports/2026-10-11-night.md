2. Tell me, and I will open it; or run `Unity.exe -projectPath D:\search-for-the-god-tree\unity -executeMethod Godtree.EditorTools.SandboxBuilder.OpenAndPlay`. That also starts the two helper programs the talking NPCs need (give them about twenty seconds).

## What I need you to decide

1. **Do the fast swings feel laggy?** The robot measured the physics hand trailing the controller by about 34 cm in a 5.5 m/s swing, with or without a weapon. I think this is what "weapons don't follow my hand" was in your first session. I have not tuned it because it needs your hands.
2. **Talking NPCs cannot ship as built.** They need two helper programs beside the game, about 5 GB of graphics memory, and one speech part is GPL (fine on your PC, not inside a released game). Try it, then decide whether the game should be designed around it.
3. **Three sandbox choices touch canon:** elemental staffs (the world bible's magic is weaving motes, with no elements), stylised blood (the art bible is bright and gentle), and things I invented to make the town live: the eight people's names and natures, and one creature (the husk wight).
4. **Licence point on your body model** (decision 0015): the rig's MIT skeleton was used to measure the weights; none of it is in the CC0 file.

## A mistake of mine to know about

For about three hours of the night (commits `1403bdc` to `f3a2909`) the robot's weapons routine was passing without checking anything: an edit of mine had removed its final check. Its report file was still written each run and I read some of them, but the commit messages from that stretch that say the routine "passes" were not backed by a check. The check is restored. With it, 41 of 43 weapons come to hand and sit still; the war scythe sagged (now lighter) and one pull in a run brought a neighbouring weapon.

## How the night's last full test run went

Twenty of twenty-one passed (the talking test skipped itself because I had stopped its helper programs to free the graphics card; it passed when they were running). The one failure was the fight: against a Barkbound and the scout together the robot landed nothing that killed in seventy strokes and was left on 11 health. Two causes, both fixed afterwards: the first creatures were too strong for a first fight, and a blade that stayed in contact with a creature only counted once. After the fix the fight passed four runs out of four (3, 4, 6 and 46 strokes). **The creatures' health, damage and pace are my guesses**; they need your hands.

## Known weak spots

- A shield held in one hand is pushed 20 to 35 degrees off if your other hand touches it. (Found when the robot's idle hand was in the way.)

- Rigged people and creatures have fingers that do not bend and mouths that do not move. Some hems are ragged.
- Creatures walk straight at you with only a sidestep for obstacles.
- Small equipment models are rough. The padded jack is a plain shell that will clip when you twist.
- The language model is small and can be talked out of character; speech recognition mishears place names.
- The robot's weapon routine has one or two odd readings per run that differ from run to run; it allows two.
- The full test suite takes about fourteen minutes.
