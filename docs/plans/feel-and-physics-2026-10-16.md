# Plan: feel and physics (audit of 2026-10-16)

From a read-only audit by an Opus agent for the lead (decision 0020: as lifelike as possible). Items marked **[guess]** are the auditor's estimates. Each item ends with the robot measurement that proves it. Status is kept in ROADMAP.md.

## Measure first
The 34 cm hand-lag figure mixes real lag with measuring error: the robot's swing (`RobotPlaytests.cs:637-642`) jumps from 0 to about 6 m/s in one frame, the gap is read before the physics step catches up, no frame rate is set, and 30 Hz sampling sees about 5 samples in a 0.18 s stroke. All routines below: `Application.targetFrameRate` 90 (repeat at 72 and 120), minimum-jerk swings with a stated peak speed, sampling at the physics rate during swings, hand compared with the joint target used in the same physics step. Re-measure the baseline this way first.

## Order: 1, 2, 5, 3, 4A, then 7, 6, 8, 9, 10, 11

1. **Hand drive tracks the controller's velocity (S-M).** `PhysicsHand.cs:26-34` sets only target position and rotation; `targetVelocity` stays zero, so the damper pulls toward standing still. Rig prefab hand joints: spring 10000, damper 500, force 5000 N; lag at constant speed = damper x v / spring = 27.5 cm at 5.5 m/s. Held objects: rotation spring 1500, damper 33.5 (`HeldObjectCollisionFilter.cs:89-94`), about 19 degrees at 15 rad/s. Fix in a new component (BIMOS is fetched, not ours to edit), `[DefaultExecutionOrder(100)]`, FixedUpdate after PhysicsHand: `joint.targetVelocity` = controller velocity minus head-body velocity (BIMOS's space), `targetAngularVelocity` from controller angular velocity, extrapolate the target to `Time.fixedTime` (cap one frame); velocity from OpenXR `deviceVelocity` or pose differencing. Interpolate both hand bodies; ContinuousSpeculative. Second hand on a shared object: no velocity tracking, half linear spring. [guess] joint-space sign convention: if lag doubles, flip. Expect: empty hand 5 cm or less at 5.5 m/s, sword twist 6 degrees or less. **Robot "HandKeepsUp":** peaks 2/4/5.5/8 m/s with empty, dagger, sword, greatsword, two-hand spear; pass empty 5 cm, sword 8 cm and 8 degrees, spear twist 15 degrees, second-hand joint force at rest 300 N or less.
2. **Real weight (M).** All weapons 0.4/0.5/0.9 kg (`SandboxBuilder.cs:620-623`); centre of mass and inertia from convex hulls (`:1288-1295`). Per-class table, explicit `centerOfMass` and `inertiaTensor` (from references, not measured): dagger 0.35 kg, balance 0.05 m, gyration radius 0.10 m; arming sword 1.1/0.12/0.25; greatsword 2.2/0.18/0.40; axe 1.3/0.45/0.15; warhammer 2.0/0.55/0.15; spear 1.8/0.30/0.50; shield 2.5/0.0. Hand linear max force 700 N; held rotation drive spring 4000, damper 60, max 40 N m one-handed. Compound colliders (axis capsule + head box) from the `ConfigureGrip` shape analysis. **Robot:** lag ranks by inertia; two-handed greatsword lags 40% less than one-handed; static sag 12 degrees or less.
3. **Physical enemy strikes, blocks and parries (M).** Now distance + a raycast at 0.55 s (`EnemyActor.cs:111-119`), held items ignored as cover (`:169-170`), carried weapon has no collider (`SandboxBuilder.cs:1144-1158`): shields block nothing. Fix: weapon or fist collider on a kinematic body moved with MovePosition, Animator in AnimatePhysics; strike window over normalised time about 0.35-0.6; contact with a player-held body = block (no damage, recoil, clang, haptic); contact with rig head/body/hands = damage scaled by weapon speed; parry = block with the player's weapon moving into it above 3 m/s, 1.2 s stagger. **Robot "ShieldBlocks":** shield in the line for 5 strikes, 0 damage and 5 block events; lowered shield takes damage. **"Parry":** a timed counter-swing staggers.
4. **Creatures react physically (A: M, B: L).** Creatures are kinematic, moved by transform in Update (`EnemyActor.cs:138-144`); damage from capped speed x mass (`:222`); one canned hurt clip (`:274`); push knot skips kinematic bodies (`SandboxAbilities.cs:397`); colliders off at death (`:289-290`). **Stage A:** move in the physics step, ground by raycast; effective impulse J = m_w M / (m_w + M) x relative speed (M 15 kg limb, 60 kg torso [guess]); knock-back dv = J / creature mass (70 kg, brute 150 kg) with 8 m/s2 friction; sprung flinch on the struck bone and spine (12 rad/s, damping 0.5, max 35 degrees) in LateUpdate; stagger above J = 6 N s with a hurt clip by direction; apply -J to the weapon; spells and knots through the same API; death ragdoll from the hit capsules (kinematic while alive, dynamic at death with the killing impulse). **Stage B** later: partial active ragdoll (about 13 bodies, [guess] 0.05-0.1 ms each). **Robot:** 5.5 m/s chest strike moves the root 5-25 cm, stagger only above threshold; push knot moves a creature 0.5 m or more; corpse settles in 3 s, nothing under the ground.
5. **Haptics (S).** None anywhere. `XRControllerWithRumble.SendImpulse`: held-object collision clamp01(J/6) for 15-60 ms; scraping proportional to sliding speed, max 0.3, every 30 ms; block or parry 0.8 for 80 ms; bow draw rising 0.05 to 0.3; release 0.6 on both hands for 30 ms; being struck 1.0 on both hands for 100 ms. The bHaptics vest uses the same events. **Robot:** the probe logs each command; every hit or block has one within a frame; none at idle.
6. **Path-finding and animal movement (M).** Runtime NavMesh, `NavMeshAgent` for the route only (updatePosition and updateRotation off); four-legged turn-rate limits (horse 90, fox 300 degrees/s), gait by speed with animator speed matched to stride, body tilt from 4 foot rays, leg IK. **Robot:** creature reaches a player behind the inn without overlapping geometry; planted feet slide under 0.1 m/s.
7. **Judder, 144 Hz physics against a 90 Hz display (S-M) [guess, measure first].** Arm IK target under a non-interpolated hand; camera under a non-interpolated pelvis (`Player.cs:26`). Interpolate; set the fixed step to a multiple of the headset rate (72 to 1/144, 90 to 1/180, 120 to 1/120) after BIMOS's Awake; update the assertion in `SandboxSceneTests.cs:29`. **Robot:** camera motion per frame varies under 5% at steady stick; grip-to-palm spread under 3 mm.
8. **Edge, point and blunt (S-M).** Everything but shields and bows is "Blade" (`SandboxBuilder.cs:1386`). Edge needs motion across the edge (dot above 0.7); point needs motion along the blade (above 0.8) within 10 cm of the tip, lodges on a limited joint, out above about 150 N [guess]; blunt staggers more, never severs. **Robot:** only the edge severs, only the thrust lodges, the hammer staggers most.
9. **Bow and arrows (S).** Arrows stick in anything (`SandboxProjectile.cs:45-52`), are turned to face their flight (`:28-29`), no drag. Stick only above 12 m/s, under 60 degrees incidence, in soft surfaces; centre of mass 0.1 m forward, linear damping about 0.015 [guess]; head hits double. Offer an "archer's aim" option (string hand to bow hand). **Robot:** 0 arrows stick in stone; grazing shots bounce; flight within 2 cm of ballistic over 20 m.
10. **Solver (S).** PGS, held-body velocity iterations 1 (`HeldObjectCollisionFilter.cs:109`). Try TGS and velocity iterations 10 on held bodies; BIMOS's jump and crouch were tuned under PGS. **Robot:** full suite; held-weapon wobble under 5 degrees/s RMS.
11. **Comfort (S each).** A speed- and turn-driven vignette, on by default, switchable; climbing prototype (BIMOS `Grab.cs:26-33` adds kinematic bodies to grabbables); feet on slopes (item 6).
