# Plan: the look (audit of 2026-10-16)

From a read-only visual audit by an Opus agent for the lead (decisions 0014 and 0020). Millisecond costs are the auditor's estimates; nothing recorded GPU time at the time. Status is kept in ROADMAP.md.

## Findings
- **The golden concepts are the wrong target.** `stylekit/golden/concepts/` was made with prompt fragments version 0, the flat storybook look decision 0014 replaced. The real target is variant F in `docs/review/session-1/style-test-env.jpg` and `style-test-chr.jpg`, and the F1 concepts in `D:\godtree-work\concepts-f1*`: soft painterly shading, little ink, a mist bank, layered blue ridges, a huge hazed tree. New golden pictures in F are needed.
- **In game:** neon lime ground everywhere, no haze (the tree reads as an oak behind a hill), hard shadows, blurry blocky textures with black cracks, spells nearly invisible in daylight.
- **Gamma colour space** (`ProjectSettings.asset`, `m_ActiveColorSpace: 0`).
- **Generated models use 6-26% of their texture** (cottage 10%, Barkbound 6.6%, innkeeper 9.3%, stone wall 14%, fountain 26%; a TripoSR sword 67%). Thousands of islands on black; black bleeds in at lower mips. `blender_finish_model.py --textured 1` never re-packs. No normal or metal/roughness maps.
- **Concept prompts ask for lighting**, which is baked into the albedo and lit again in the engine.
- **`Godtree/Toon` is on everything** (correction: no standard Lit on the environment), but it uses only the main light (campfire and spell lights do nothing), ignores the ambient set in `BuildLook`, has no highlights (metal reads as chalk), no AO input or DepthNormals pass, a rim on the lit side (art bible rule 3 says away from the sun), and back faces of two-sided characters are not flipped.
- **URP:** soft shadows off, one cascade at 2048 over 50 m, no renderer features, no depth texture, MSAA 4x (right for VR), single-pass instanced, foveation off.
- **Post:** Neutral tonemapping, contrast +9, saturation +14, bloom 0.4 at 0.95, linear fog 35-420 m.
- **Ground texture** 1024 px and mirror-symmetric: rosettes repeat.
- **Effects:** additive glow particles (blood glows like embers), bolt trails, unlit spell spheres, point lights. No dust, weapon trails or water. Text is legacy bitmap `TextMesh`, blurry in a headset.

## Budget
11.1 ms per frame at 90 fps, about 9 ms of GPU after the compositor. Keep the 5090 at about 6 ms or less so mid-high PCs (2.5-3x slower, estimate) hold 90. The talking-NPC helpers share the GPU (about 5 GB) and their spikes drop frames.

## Order
0. **GPU and CPU frame times** in the recordings (the game-state probe does this).
1. **Linear colour space**, then re-tune: shadow tint start (0.62, 0.60, 0.86), contrast 4, saturation 0, Shadows/Midtones/Highlights cool shadows and warm highlights, white balance +6. 0 ms. Lead only (ProjectSettings).
2. **Re-pack and re-bake texture atlases** (Smart UV + Pack Islands with rotation, margin 0.003; bake from the original, 16 px margin; dilate). Remove lighting words from the image-to-3D fragment (keep "flat, even, shadowless lighting"). Expect 3-8x texture detail. 0 ms.
3. **`Godtree/Toon` v2**, one shared include for toon, ground and hills: additional lights on the same ramp (`_ADDITIONAL_LIGHTS`, `_FORWARD_PLUS`); ambient from `SampleSH(normal)`; rim masked away from the sun (x `saturate(-dot(L,V))*0.6+0.4`, power 4, strength 0.35); optional ramp texture; stylised highlight plus matcap for metal (`_Metal`, `_Gloss`) with geometric anti-aliasing; flip back-face normals; DepthNormals pass and `_SCREEN_SPACE_OCCLUSION`. URP: soft shadows Medium, two cascades split 0.15, 4096, 60 m. About 0.6-0.8 ms. References: NiloCat `UnityURPToonLitShaderExample` (MIT), MToon in UniVRM (MIT); match MToon's parameter names.
4. **Atmosphere and the Godtree's scale:** shared `GodtreeAtmosphere.hlsl` in Toon, Ground, Sky and Backdrop: exponential height fog (density 0.004, falloff 0.08) and aerial perspective (warm toward the sun, cool away); two or three ridge silhouettes at 1.5 and 3 km (haze 0.3 and 0.55); a mist band at the tree's base and a cloud card across the canopy; side-light sun, try `Euler(38, 110, 0)` [guess]. Under 0.1 ms. No ray-marched volumetrics in VR yet.
5. **VR image quality:** keep MSAA 4x (check STP/TAA XR support before considering); render scale 1.3 as a quality option (about 1.5-2.5 ms); anisotropic 16x on ground and buildings; alpha-to-coverage for foliage; fade rim and highlight with `fwidth(normal)`; TextMeshPro text, cap height at least 20 mm at 1 m; no chromatic aberration, depth of field or motion blur.
6. **Ambient occlusion and contact:** URP SSAO from depth-normals, half resolution, radius 0.3, intensity 0.6, tinted to the shadow colour; blob shadows under moving characters; or AO baked into vertex colour in Blender. 0.5-1.0 ms.
7. **Ground and grass:** a non-symmetric 2048 tile, less saturated, macro variation; GPU-instanced anime grass tufts within 30 m with wind and tip gradient, bending from the player's feet. 0.5-1.0 ms.
8. **Foliage:** leaf-card trees with normals from an ellipsoid proxy, alpha-to-coverage, flutter 0.02 m at 3 Hz. About 0.2 ms.
9. **Effects:** alpha-blended dark-edged shape plus additive HDR core (3-4); soft particles; blade trails above 3 m/s; dust on footfalls and impacts; 30-50 ms hit flash; blood as alpha-blended red-pink droplets, not glowing. Kenney Particle Pack (CC0) textures.
10. **Outlines** (optional): inverted hull on characters, enemies and held weapons; albedo x 0.4; about 1.2 mm at 1 m; fade past 15 m; needs smoothed normals.
11. **Faces:** their own UV region at 512 px or more and a face shadow map; players on VRM with MToon.
12. **Smaller:** reflection probes, adaptive probe volumes for bounce light, stylised water with foam for the fountain.
13. **Spatial audio:** Steam Audio (Apache-2.0); check how sounds are spatialised now.
