# Proposals

Any contributor may pitch a creature, quest, mechanic or zone.

1. Open a proposal issue (the template asks the right questions).
2. For anything longer than the issue, add one Markdown file here: `proposals/<issue-number>-<short-name>.md`.
3. The lead accepts, reshapes or declines, always with a reason. Accepted proposals become canon changes plus task briefs.

Ideas that surprise us are welcome. Ideas that break a [pillar](../canon/pillars.md) are declined, however good they are in isolation. Proposals may add beliefs about the god in the Godtree; they may not settle what is there.
