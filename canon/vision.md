# Vision

Status: Approved by the owner, 2026-10-09 (decisions/0008).

## One sentence

You wake in a bright world with a colossal tree on the horizon, a god is said to live in it, and you walk there with your friends, fighting with your own hands.

## The game

*Search for the Godtree* is a co-operative PC VR action role-playing game.

- **The journey is the game.** The world is a chain of zones leading toward the Godtree. Each zone has a town, a wilderness, a dungeon, and a boss that gates the next zone. Zones are built and released one at a time.
- **It feels like an online world without being one.** A shared hub town holds 16 to 20 players. Parties of 4 to 8 leave it together into their own copy of a zone. One player's PC hosts the party.
- **Combat is physical.** Weapons have weight. Hits, blocks and parries come from simulated collisions, never from canned animations. Enemies are physically simulated too.
- **Roles come from what you carry.** Melee weapons (swords, spears, axes, daggers, shields), bows, and magic cast with hand gestures. Tank, healer and damage roles emerge from gear and skills; nobody picks a class from a menu.
- **Movement is free and comfortable.** Smooth thumbstick locomotion with comfort options, teleport as an alternative, and climbing, gliding and dashing as core traversal.
- **It looks like a bright cel-shaded anime.** Readable silhouettes and strong colour matter more than realism.
- **The tone is adventure and wonder**, with real danger in dungeons and boss fights.

## What it is not

- Not a flat-screen or standalone-headset game. PC VR only, through OpenXR.
- Not an MMO. No persistent shared servers, no subscription, no economy to police.
- Not a story about being trapped in a game. The fiction never says the world is a game, there is no log-out plot and no death game. The Woken simply live here.
- Not a tribute. The inspiration is anime about players living inside a VR online world, but this world is original. No names, characters, places, designs, logos or story elements from *Sword Art Online* or any other existing work appear in it, and contributions that use them are declined.

## Where we deliberately differ from our inspirations

A giant tree with a quest to reach it is an old mythic idea and also appears in existing anime. To stay clearly our own:

- The journey is overland, on foot, zone by zone. It is not a race to fly up a tree, and there are no winged fairy races.
- Nobody knows what is in the Godtree, and the game does not answer early. Each land believes something different.
- Our vocabulary is our own (Woken, Cradles, the Rootway, weaving). See the world bible.

## Who it is for

Players with a gaming PC and a PC-connected headset who want to swing a real-feeling sword next to friends, and who would rather explore a hopeful world than a grim one.
