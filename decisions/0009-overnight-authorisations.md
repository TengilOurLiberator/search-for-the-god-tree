# 0009: Licence acceptances, in-house ragdolls, standing permissions

- **Date:** 2026-10-09
- **Decided by:** Owner

**Decisions.**
1. **VRoid Studio** may be used for placeholder avatars. They live in their own folder with a notice that pixiv's terms apply, and are excluded from the CC0 dedication. A fully CC0 avatar route is still to be tested in milestone 3.
2. **Stable Audio Open** is approved. The Stability AI Community License is free under US $1,000,000 annual revenue and requires registration for commercial use. **The owner must register with Stability AI before any commercial release**; the lead cannot create accounts.
3. **Chatterbox** is approved, accepting that every output carries an inaudible watermark. (The lead recommended against; Kokoro remains the default for voices.)
4. **Active ragdolls are written in-house.** `BIMOS-Active-Ragdolls` is not the owner's repository and has no licence; its code is not to be used or read for reference.
5. **The lead may commit and push its own work to `main` without waiting**, including restricted areas. Outside contributions stay closed, and merges of other people's work still follow the rules in docs/review.md.
6. **Pacing.** Spend the weekly usage budget across the whole week, not early. When it runs low, schedule local generation on the owner's PC, which needs no budget.

**Affects.** `data/approved-generators.json` (statuses), THIRD_PARTY_LICENCES.md, ROADMAP.md, docs/lead.md.
