# 0016: Owner's direction after the fourth headset session

- **Date:** 2026-10-10 (late evening)
- **Decided by:** Owner

After playing the evening build the owner said it was "okay" and set the next work. These are sandbox prototypes unless a later decision makes them canon.

1. **Staffs and wands cast.** Each staff or wand has an element. More powerful ones of the same element have more and bigger spells.
2. **Arrows got stuck when shooting.** Fixed the same night: every arrow struck the bow it was shot from.
3. **People and creatures are animated**, and regenerated for it (slimmer, no loose cloth at first).
4. **Free AI conversation with every NPC**, now: the player speaks, a language model running on the PC answers in character with a generated voice. The lead recommended written dialogue first; the owner chose AI conversation. Known costs, accepted: it shares the graphics card with VR, players need a strong PC, and NPCs can say things nobody wrote.
5. **The lead playtests alone.** A robot player drives the rig without a headset (`Scripts/Diagnostics/RobotPlayer.cs`, `Tests/PlayMode/RobotPlaytests.cs`).
6. **The sandbox becomes a small town with an outside area for monsters.** The town is Huskhaven as written in the world bible.
7. **Monsters are dismembered when killed** (arms, legs, head cut off), with **stylised blood**. The lead recommended splinters and sap to suit the bright look and keep the age rating low; the owner chose stylised blood. This touches the tone of the game: the art bible and vision should be checked against it before it becomes canon.
8. **New free tools found by search may be installed and used overnight** if the lead has read the licence and it allows commercial use. Anything doubtful waits for the owner. Survey: `docs/research/tools-survey-2026-10-10.md`.
9. **The PC runs all night.**
