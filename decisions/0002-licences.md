# 0002: Project licences

- **Date:** 2026-10-09
- **Decided by:** Owner

**Decision.** Code is MIT. Assets, recipes, documentation and canon text are CC0 1.0.

**Reason.** MIT is simple and compatible with the stack. CC0 suits generated work, whose copyright status is uncertain in many countries, and needs no attribution bookkeeping across many contributors.

**Affects.** `LICENSE`, `LICENSE-ASSETS.md`, `data/licences.json`, the recipe `licence` field. Third-party packages keep their own licences and are never vendored.

**Open.** VRoid-made avatars cannot be CC0 (see THIRD_PARTY_LICENCES.md). The owner has not yet ruled on how avatars are handled.
